# Code of conduct

TempDrop contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, welcoming, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed in good faith with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.
