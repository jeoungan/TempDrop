IMPORTANT: PERMANENT FILE DELETION

TempDrop permanently deletes expired files from its dedicated managed folder.
Deleted files do not go to the Recycle Bin and cannot be restored by TempDrop.

Deletion remains disabled until first launch, when the user must:

1. Select a dedicated managed folder.
2. Select a retention period.
3. Explicitly accept the permanent-deletion warning.

Uninstalling TempDrop removes its scheduled task but preserves the managed folder,
configuration, and remaining files. TempDrop does not upload files or send telemetry.
